    ABOUT

    Paranoid Reverb

    Paranoid Reverb are a five-piece heavy psych rock band from Thessaloniki, Greece, formed in 2018.

    Built around heavy riffs, hypnotic grooves and psychedelic textures, their sound moves between raw weight and more atmospheric, dynamic passages, with the energy of the live performance remaining at its core.

    The band released their debut full-length, Billion Drops, in 2020. In 2026, Paranoid Reverb return with their second album, Red River Black Roots, pushing their sound into darker and more expansive territory while keeping the physical, live-first energy at the centre of the band.
